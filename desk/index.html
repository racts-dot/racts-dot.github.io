      What you achieved

      How sure

      Every fact on this desk carries the day it was read. This is how far to trust where it came from.

      Measured

      Somebody opened the thing itself this session: the Etsy dashboard, the bank export, the built file. The strongest kind.

      News report

      Reporters writing down what happened, like most AFR stories. Reliable on facts, not on predictions.

      Data from a platform

      Etsy search ranks, eBay sold counts, YouTube views. True on the day read, and they move.

      Forecast

      A bank or economist predicting the future. Useful direction, often wrong on the number.

      Two AIs agreed

      GPT and Gemini reviewed the same packet blind and agreed. Good for spotting weak points, not proof the market wants it.

      Opinion or creator

      A columnist or a YouTube creator. One person's case, often selling something. Try it before believing it.

      Reported

      Second-hand: another chat or a note said so and nobody re-checked. Check before acting on it.

      Market whisper

      Insiders talking before anything is announced (AFR Street Talk). Likely, not confirmed.

      How research is done here

      1. Numbers first, before anything is made: a named buyer, a demand figure with the date it was read, real competitors with prices.
      2. Open the top three competitors and list what each one actually hands over.
      3. Two AIs review blind. Where they disagree, a third round decides.
      4. Anything going live is re-measured from the source by a separate check.
