RECIPE 09 — Plan mode 90% of the time, and gates on your words

From: How I Get 30M+ Monthly Views with Claude Code + Blotato · 17 Feb 2026 · 1:12:20 · watch

**She calls it her "ultimate Claude Code free course", built on "over 500 hours using Claude Code on a regular basis." The Blotato half you can skip. The Claude Code half is the most useful hour of the twelve.**

⭐⭐ THE ONE THING — plan mode, 90% of the time

"I spend about 90% of my time in plan mode so that Claude doesn't accidentally change anything. And then when I'm very happy with a plan, then I switch to edit automatically." "we spend 90% of our time back and forth sparring back and forth on the plan. And then only when I'm super happy with it and confident it's going to not break stuff, then I switch."

Why this matters more to you than to almost anyone

Your usage report, measured this week:

   12  sessions where I made EXCESSIVE CHANGES
   20  sessions where I took the WRONG APPROACH
   22  misunderstood requests
        │
   your own report: "Over-production annoys you
   more than under-delivery"

⭐ Plan mode is the mechanical answer to exactly that. It is a switch at the bottom left that stops me touching anything until you have agreed what I am about to do.

   HOW IT GOES NOW              HOW SHE WORKS
   ───────────────              ─────────────
   you ask                      you ask
   I go and do it               I write the plan
        │                            │
   you find I did too much     you argue with the plan
        │                            │
   you ask me to revert        THEN I do it

⚠ She does add one caveat: for a small build from scratch you can be "a little more aggressive". Her 90% figure is for complex existing work — which is what yours is.

Her plan advice — worth knowing before your next bill

"I am personally on the $200 per month plan... If you are just on the $20 per month plan, you are going to hit the limits very quickly because of all the tool calls involved when you use an agentic tool like Claude Code. So I recommend at minimum this $100 per month plan."

⭐ And her honest counterweight: "you will find that you will be able to cancel so many different tools once you actually pick up Claude Code."

⛔ That is money, so it is yours. I am reporting her figures, not recommending a plan.

One habit she ends the course with

"your memory as you do these projects — adding them to your CLAUDE.md, the things you've learned, tips and tricks that are helpful... make sure to add that to your CLAUDE.md at the end of every session."

⚠ You do this already, heavily. ⭐ But note what she says it is FOR: things learned, not rules imposed. Your file has drifted toward the second.
