The four things you are probably NOT using

1 · Dispatch — run it from your phone, reaching your computer

"you could be waiting at a doctor's office or waiting in line somewhere and tell Cowork, 'grab the last three photos I took or grab something from my downloads folder and post it to LinkedIn.'"

⚠ This is the one both reviewers split on today. GPT flagged that its relationship to your protected Remote Control setup is unresolved. Do not set it up blind.

2 · Skills are composable

"you can have one skill that knows it should use a bunch of other skills."

⚠ Both reviewers said DROP this for now — chaining removes the moment where you check the first skill's output before the second eats it.

3 · Ideas — Anthropic's own suggestion list

"if you really have no idea where to start, I recommend just going to ideas."

⚠ Both reviewers said drop this too: a panel that suggests more things to do is the last thing for someone with a backlog.

4 · "Allow" vs "Always allow"

"If you're a beginner to this, I recommend clicking allow, not always allow... AI has access to your computer, which is powerful but potentially dangerous."

⚠ She then clicks "always allow" herself for filming and says not to copy her. ✅ You audited this today: 31 auto-approved, zero blocked.

⚠ What to ignore

All five of her use cases run on Blotato — her own $29/month product. The social-calendar section is a demo, and you do not post to social media as a business.

⭐ One technical detail worth keeping: local files went straight from her computer to the publishing tool. "You don't have to use an intermediate storage solution like Google Drive, Dropbox, or Amazon S3." That four-step dance is a real annoyance in many tools.
