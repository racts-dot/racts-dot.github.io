THE SEVEN

1 · ultra think — think hard, just this once

Type the words ultra think inside a prompt.

"regardless of your current model and your current reasoning effort... Claude knows that you want it to spend the maximum amount of time thinking and reasoning."

Why: leave everything on a cheap setting, spend the effort only where it counts. "to save tokens especially... you can do all of your normal work in your default setting low or medium."

2 · /caveman — make Claude shut up

An open-source skill. To get it: "Just open Claude and be like, 'Hey, I want to install this open source skill called caveman.' And it will just do it for you."

"it will help cut a significant percentage of output tokens instantly."

⭐ The one that directly protects your allowance. Every word I write back spends it.

3 · /insights — a school report on how you use Claude

"Claude will analyze all of your sessions in the past 30 days... How are you being productive using Claude? Kind of what's holding you back? Anti-patterns."

A detailed HTML report, based on what you actually do, not generic advice. Her advice: once a month.

✅ You ran this today. It produced the 139-session report with the 12 excessive-changes and 20 wrong-approach figures that several of these recipes now reference.

4 · /loop — repeat a job on your own computer ✅ EXISTS IN YOUR SETUP

   /loop   5m   /write a draft post
     │      │        │
   command interval  what to do

Shortest interval 1 minute · expires after 3 days · ⚠ needs your computer ON and the session open.

6 · /btw — ask a side question without interrupting ⭐ THE ONE FOR YOU

"you can just type /by the way, answer this other question. And Claude will basically continue doing its main thing over here and also answer your question... without interrupting the main task."

⭐ Why it matters for you: you have a long conversation built up with me. A fresh window loses all of it. "instead of having to open a new session which is lacking that context, you can run /btw."

She calls it her favourite. "pretty much everybody will find it universally useful."

7 · /clear — wipe the memory between jobs

   1. QUALITY   "AI becomes less accurate if there's a
                 ton of stuff already in the context"
   2. COST      it saves tokens — your allowance

When she uses it: "when I switch between tasks... writing a YouTube script and now I'm done, switching to a TikTok script that has nothing to do with it."

⚠ It really does delete the conversation. Do not run it mid-job.
