RECIPE 05 — How she got 1.4M followers working one day a week

From: I built a 1.4M personal brand that drives 1.2M website views to my startup · 27 Sep 2025 · 36:39 · watch

⭐ Of the twelve you flagged, this is the one that speaks to your actual problem. It is also the least product-demo of the lot.

Why this one matters more than the others

Your own review board, twice, said the same thing:

"You have built a flawless system for producing things nobody has asked for." "There is no evidenced customer hypothesis."

25 listings. 142 views. 0 sales. No audience anywhere.

This video is a playbook for exactly that gap, written by someone who did it solo, with no ads, no team, and one working day a week.

1 · The claim that reframes everything

"with ChatGPT and generative AI, it is now super easy to have AI write a pretty good sounding post. So how do you stand out when everyone is using ChatGPT to create content?"

Her answer:

"people care most about the results you can demonstrate... The more concrete the results you can share, the more credibility you will have."

⭐ And the bit for someone with no results yet — which is you, today

"It's really, really simple. Just document your journey and your goal. So if your goal is to make 20,000 per month with your new product, just state that and then document what you're doing along the way."
"A common mistake I see is that content creators... focus a bit too much just on the content and not enough on actually doing meaningful stuff that people want to follow you for. They want to follow your journey. They want to follow along with what you're learning, with what you're trying, experimenting with, what's failing."

⭐⭐ You have been documenting a journey all year without publishing a word of it. Your files are full of measured failures, corrected numbers, and things that did not work. That is the raw material she says people follow.

2 · Her constraints — read these before the tactics

She lists them so the results cannot be misread:

   NO paid ads            NO collaborations
   NO team                NO paid posts
   NO video editors       NO affiliate deals
   ─────────────────────────────────────────
   CONTENT: ONE DAY PER WEEK
   Nothing promoted that she earns from,
   except her own product
"my strategy mostly consists of saying no to a lot of things."

She says no to 99.9% of: meetings, podcasts, advisory, coaching, consulting, investments, partnerships, mentorship, speaking.

"one of the most common pitfalls I see is people trying to do way too much... trying to build three different businesses at the same time while also building a personal brand and two faceless channels as well. Like that's ridiculous to me. You should just choose one thing and say no to everything else."

⚠ Worth sitting with. Printables, eBay, the car rental, the transcript archive, the learning system.

3 · The funnel — and it is three boxes, not a diagram

   TOP     short form      TikTok, Instagram, Shorts, Threads, Twitter
              │
   MIDDLE  longer form     LinkedIn, Substack Notes, YouTube
              │
   BOTTOM  ⭐ EMAIL LIST   118,000 subscribers · 40% open rate
                           zero ad spend · no team · one email a week
"whether or not you're building a separate product, if you are a creator... email is going to be your number one highest ROI channel. Like, for sure, even for most businesses, email is the channel."

⭐ One link. Not a Linktree.

"if you check the bio of all of my social media profiles, they all direct you to my newsletter... I don't want to confuse people with five billion links."
"you want to own your audience outside of social platforms... you want to be able to reach them whenever you feel like it, not dictated by the latest algorithm updates."

⭐⭐ And the only number she checks

"The only metric I actually look at every month is whether my email newsletter list is growing. As long as it's growing, I know that my engine is working. And honestly, I don't stress over all the little things that are broken and messed up."

One number. Monthly. Everything else is noise.

⚠ You currently have no email list and no equivalent single number. Your shop stats are a proxy for nothing you control.

4 · Original content on ONE OR TWO platforms — everything else is repurposed

"I focus on making high-quality original content on one to two platforms. YouTube for long form content, TikTok for short form content. Then I use a combination of AI and automation to repurpose my original content to multiple platforms."
"I don't change the strategy every other month. It's all about like am I executing enough and am I executing at a high quality? Usually when there's an issue like my numbers are down, the issue is with volume or quality or both."

⭐ Her best-performing YouTube video was a repurposed TikTok — "I took one viral TikTok and just talked for three more minutes about the same topic."

5 · What actually works, by platform — her observations

PlatformWhat works
TikTok / InstagramChatGPT prompts — "broadly applicable", far more people understand a prompt than an automation. Listicles — "people love simple listicles where you have done the work of curating things that are valuable." Making money with AI
LinkedInLead-magnet giveaways. Lead with a result, then "comment this and I'll send you a playbook." One post: 1,200 comments, 85,000 impressions
SubstackQuote cards. "turning into Instagram"
YouTubeWorkflow automations, vibe coding

⚠ Her honest note on the "comment and I'll send you" tactic

"a lot of people find this format very cringey, but in my opinion, it depends how frequently you're doing it and the quality of the results you're claiming as well as the quality of the actual playbook you're delivering."
"if you do this every single day, it's unlikely that what you're sending people is actually going to provide value. But if you do this once a week or once every other week, and you put a lot of time and effort into creating a valuable, helpful playbook... I don't think it's cringey at all."

⭐ Earlier I measured that 161 of her videos use "free" as this hook — about 1 in 10 of everything she posts. That is roughly once a week. She does what she says here.

⭐ What to actually do — and it is one thing, not a strategy

Do not build a funnel. Write down the mission and the number.

   1. ONE SENTENCE: what are you trying to do,
      and by when
      (hers: "teach 10 million people AI")

   2. ONE NUMBER you check monthly
      (hers: is the email list growing)

   3. ONE THING you say yes to.
      Everything else gets a no.

⚠ That costs nothing and needs no tool. And on the evidence of your own review board, it is the thing that has been missing all year — not another product, not better SEO.
