⭐ THE IDEA — a writing gate that refuses to publish

She does not ask Claude to "write a good post". She makes it grade its own work against a fixed rubric and rewrite until it passes.

   WHAT MOST PEOPLE DO          WHAT SHE DOES
   ───────────────────          ─────────────
   "write me a post"            write it
        │                            ▼
   read it, shrug,              grade it against a rubric
   post it                           ▼
                                score too low? ──► rewrite
                                     │              │
                                     ▼              ▼
                                pre-publish check ◄─┘
                                     │
                                ⛔ fails a rule = NOT PUBLISHED
"The post grader uses a viral rubric to determine the virality of your post, and it's going to keep iterating on your post until it passes a certain score or a certain grade."

Her actual pre-publish rules — copy these, they cost nothing

"No M dashes, no banned words, using active voice and contractions for viral copywriting. No hashtags because it's Twitter... And then under the character limit."
RuleWhy
No em dashesthe single clearest AI tell
A banned-words listhers is long; this is a "starting point"
Active voice + contractionsreads human, not corporate
Platform-specific hashtag rule"I only want hashtags on Instagram and TikTok"
Under the character limitmechanical, checkable

⭐ Every one of those is checkable by a plain script. No AI needed to enforce them.

Her three hook frameworks — she writes all three, then picks

Watching it work on a real post, it produced:

FrameworkThe actual line
Receipts"30 million views in 30 days"
Reverse hook"everyone says pick one platform, I pick six"
Specific numbers"Six platforms, 30 days, 30 million views"

It chose the reverse hook "because it's polarizing and we're posting on Twitter."

⭐ Three drafts against three named frameworks, then a reasoned pick. That is transferable to an Etsy title, a product description, anything.

⭐ The working habit worth stealing — the screenshot folder

"Throughout the week, you just take photos or screenshots of cool stuff. You drop it into a folder. And then when you sit down to make content, you tell Claude, 'Hey, go analyze all of the photos and screenshots I took throughout the week and let's make content out of them.'"

Collect all week without thinking. Make things in one sitting. Costs nothing, needs no tool.

One small prompt detail worth keeping

When putting text on an image for vertical video:

"respecting social media safe zones for short-form vertical content"
"This is really important because if you don't do this, then it's going to add text way too close to the edges, and it just looks weird."

⭐ A five-word phrase that prevents a whole class of ugly output.

What to try — free, and it points at your real business

Do not build her social system. Build the gate, for your listings.

   1. write down your OWN banned-words list
      (start with: em dashes, "elevate", "unleash",
       "transform", "perfect for")

   2. next time you write listing copy, ask for
      THREE versions against three named angles

   3. then ask it to grade all three against your
      list and say which fails what

⚠ You already discovered today that two of your listings made claims the product did not deliver — a title page promising 540 meanings that delivered none, and a description saying 25 pages when the file held 23. A pre-publish word gate is the thing that catches that class of error.
