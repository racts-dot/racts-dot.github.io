RECIPE 08 — Her entire business stack, in three and a half minutes

**From: Every Tool I Use to Run My Multimillion Dollar Business Solo · 25 Mar 2026 · 3:27 ·** watch

The shortest of the twelve and the densest. A whole multi-million-pound solo business, listed.

🚨 First — this corrects something I told you today

This morning I said n8n had gone cold, because mentions in her video titles dropped to zero in August and September.

This video shows she RUNS her customer support on it:

"the actual brain of my AI support agent running 24/7 lives in self-hosted n8n."
   WHAT I MEASURED            WHAT IT ACTUALLY MEANT
   ───────────────            ──────────────────────
   n8n in TITLES              she stopped making
   went to zero               VIDEOS about it
        │                            │
   I said "it's obsolete"     she still runs her

   ⛔ WRONG

⭐ "Stopped talking about it" is not "stopped using it." A creator's title counts measure what gets views, not what they use. I over-read my own number and I am correcting it here.

⚠ The practical version stands: if you were learning automation today, her own newer videos point at Claude Code, not n8n. But calling n8n obsolete was too strong.

The stack, by area

Money and legal

CompanyDelaware C Corp, set up via Stripe Atlas
BillingStripe
BankMercury — "really startup friendly, super modern design"

Website and customers

WebsiteWebflow — "not amazing", a $50 template, filled in the text and images, "really haven't changed it since I launched"
Getting customersher own app
SupportIntercom for the widget and email, with the actual agent brain in self-hosted n8n

⭐ The website line is the useful one. A multi-million business runs on a fifty-dollar template she has not touched since launch.

Building things

CodingClaude Code — "my primary coding tool... over a year, far longer than most AI influencers"
EditorEmacs — "I'm a masochist"
BrowserChrome + Vimium (keyboard navigation) + Claude in Chrome to automate tasks

⚠ Note the date. This is March. By August she had switched to Codex (Recipe 06). Not a contradiction — a change over five months. ⭐ Worth remembering when you read any tool recommendation: it has a use-by date.

⭐ Keeping track — and you already do this

Ideas and memoriesa Google Doc she calls her diary — "where I just dump different ideas and different memories"
Technical tasksGitHub projects Kanban, tied to her code

⭐ One loose place to dump thinking, one strict place for tasks. That is your HANDOVER.md and your Notion, in different clothes.

Making content

Short videoTikTok Studio — "It's free, you get native looking TikTok captions, it's easy"
Long videoDescript (she also names Teleport TV and Screen Studio)
LiveOBS, open source
DM automationManyChat — and ⭐ "Claude in Chrome is what actually creates my ManyChat DM automations automatically"
DictationVoice Inq — chosen because "you only have to pay once, it's not a recurring subscription fee"

Desk

Stressless recliner ("best home office purchase ever"), Glove 80 ergonomic keyboard, two monitors stacked vertically, MacBook Pro for portability, Mac Studio as the main machine.

⭐ What she does NOT have — the most interesting list

"Other things I surprisingly don't have are a lawyer, insurance and employees."

And, in her own joke list: no hairbrush, no makeup, no dress.

   A MULTI-MILLION BUSINESS WITH
   ─────────────────────────────
   no employees      no lawyer
   no insurance      no agency
   no paid ads       no video editor

⚠ Not advice — a data point. She is describing what she has chosen to go without, not telling you to.

What is worth taking

   1. THE $50 TEMPLATE
      her website is a bought template, untouched since launch.
      Yours does not need to be better than that.

   2. THE TWO-PLACE SYSTEM
      one loose diary for thinking, one strict board for tasks.
      You have both already.

   3. PAY-ONCE OVER SUBSCRIBE
      she picked her dictation tool on that basis alone.

⛔ Do not copy the stack. Half of it is for a SaaS company with paying subscribers. You sell files.
