# Tap a batch, see what is in it — 11 September 2026

## Done. Every batch row opens a floating box now.

https://claude.ai/code/artifact/dfca84a1-19e9-4be9-bfd3-9b906ea52e79

Tap **All batches**, then tap any row. A box floats over the page with every
item in that batch. Tap outside it, press Escape, or use the Close button to get
back. Nothing moves, nothing reloads, and you do not lose your place in the
table.

---

## What the box shows

Across the top, six figures for that batch alone.

| | |
|---|---|
| Items | how many things were in that shopping trip |
| Sold | how many have found a buyer |
| Kept | how many you kept for yourself |
| Spent | what the trip cost |
| Got back | what has come back in |
| Difference | the two subtracted, red if you are down |

Underneath, every item in order, exactly as it looks in the main list — the
name, what it cost, what it sold for if it has, the buyer, and where it is up
to. The coloured stripe down the left still means the same thing: green sold,
amber ready to list, blue on its way, grey kept.

---

## Small thing worth knowing

The row header now also names the device the batch was bought on and the store
credit that paid for it — for example *bought on the Galaxy S25 Edge, ordered
11 Jul, $300 store credit*. That was in your spreadsheet all along and the table
had no room for it.

---

## Recap

Your ledger app now opens a floating box when you tap a batch, showing every
item in that batch with its own six figures at the top. It works by tapping,
by keyboard, and closes on Escape or by tapping outside. Nothing else about the
app changed.
