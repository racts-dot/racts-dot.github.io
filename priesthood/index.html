Priest Hood

How to read this. Each sentence says only what the verses beside it say. Tap a reference to read the verse, in the World English Bible or the King James Version. Words in Hebrew, Aramaic and Greek show the form, a transliteration, the Strong's number and a short gloss from the STEP Bible lexicon. Anything that comes from outside the 66 books of the Protestant Bible, or from later tradition, is in a box marked Outside Scripture.

In this build: God's names 27, Angels 20, Demons and devils 23, Elements from the Scriptures 16, Ordinances of heaven 8 entries; 349 verse references.
