Privacy policy

For the browser extension Start · 19 September 2026

Start collects nothing. It has no analytics, no accounts, no advertising and no third-party services. Nothing you do in it is sent to the developer, because there is nowhere for it to be sent.

What it stores, and where

Start is a replacement new tab page. Two things it holds are yours:

  • Today's focus line stays in your own browser, on that one device. It is never transmitted anywhere.
  • The scratch pad is saved to your own Notion workspace, through a relay that you run, so that the same note appears on your other computers. It passes through no service belonging to anyone else.

What it connects to

Exactly two addresses, both belonging to the person using it:

  • racts-dot.github.io — four small JSON files are read to display the day's progress, decisions waiting, and the list of links shown on the page.
  • apps-notion-relay.apps-notion-relay.workers.dev — the scratch pad note is read and written here, and this relay is operated by the user and speaks only to the user's own Notion.

No other address is contacted. The extension does not read the pages you browse, does not touch your history, your bookmarks, your passwords or your other tabs, and does not run any code fetched from the internet.

Who can see it

Only you. The developer has no server, receives no copy of anything, and has no way to read your focus line or your scratch pad.

Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension removes the focus line with the browser's own storage. The scratch pad lives in your Notion workspace, so delete that page to remove it.

Children

Start is a personal productivity page. It is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If the extension ever starts handling data differently, this page will say so, with the date it changed.

Questions about this policy go to the publisher contact address listed on the extension's Chrome Web Store page.
The extension's full source is at Start-extension.zip, so every claim here can be checked rather than taken on trust.
