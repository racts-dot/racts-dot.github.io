## The one habit that matters now

**Only ever work in this folder.**

The trouble this week was two copies drifting apart, and it nearly cost you
TripShare — 1,210 lines that existed in one place and had never been sent up. One
copy makes that impossible.

If you ever need it on another computer, do not copy the folder across. Pull a
fresh one from GitHub instead — that keeps a single shared history rather than
two that can disagree.
