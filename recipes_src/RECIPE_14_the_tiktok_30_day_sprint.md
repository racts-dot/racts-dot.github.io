# ⭐⭐ THE ONE THING — TikTok tells you what people search for and cannot find

Her name for it is Creator Search Insights. It shows what people type into TikTok search, with numbers, and a "content gap" tab for searches that have too few videos.

> "Tik Tok is literally asking you to make content on those topics so it can serve it to the people searching for it" ([14:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I3bOOiJirgk&t=870s))

Her path, February 2025 ([15:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I3bOOiJirgk&t=900s)):

```
TikTok app ─► Home ─► search bar (top right)
            ─► type "creator search insights" ─► View
            ─► Trending tab   (what millions search)
            ─► Content gap    (searched, not enough videos)
            ─► type your own keyword in its search bar
            ─► bookmark the phrases you like
```

Then the rule that she says moved her from about 200 views to thousands: use the exact phrase everywhere. Title, description, hashtags, and said out loud ([07:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I3bOOiJirgk&t=451s)).

Her check that it worked: open your own video and look at the search bar at the top.

> "you don't want it to say find related Concept in the top search bar here" ([07:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I3bOOiJirgk&t=420s))

She ran it on seven videos in two days. The captions read "23 9,000 views in the past 48 hours" ([18:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I3bOOiJirgk&t=1113s)). That is probably 239,000, but auto-captions garble numbers, so treat it as unverified. ⚠ It was also on an AI account that already had a large following.

## Why this matters to you

Your listings can already be found. You rank #2 on Etsy AU for "dementia caregiver binder". What is unproven is what happens after someone sees a listing.

```
WHAT THE SPRINT FIXES         WHAT YOU HAVEN'T PROVEN
─────────────────────         ───────────────────────
nobody sees you               people see the listing
        │                             │
more videos, more views       ...and do not buy
        │                             │
exposure                      the words and the offer
```

⚠ So a TikTok sprint pushes on exposure, which is not your measured gap. Be honest about that before spending hours.

⭐ But the search tool helps with the other side. It shows the exact words caregivers type, with numbers, for free. That is buyer language for titles and first lines, gathered before you build or rewrite anything.

## ⚠ The honest time sum

Her own figure: "usually doesn't take me more than 15, max 20 minutes per video" ([22:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T-F_2Kga3Os&t=1320s)). That is her speed, as a creator with 680,000 followers.

```
100 videos × 15–20 min filming   = 25–33 hours
100 posts  × 15–30 min replying  = 25–50 hours
                                   ───────────
in 30 days                       ≈ 50–83 hours
                                 ≈ 1.7–2.8 hours a day
```

⛔ That does not fit a full-time job. She says so herself:

> "if Tik Tok is not going to be your primary Channel then you don't need to do this" ([08:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I3bOOiJirgk&t=480s))

## ⚠ Where the two videos disagree, or overclaim

- Shadowban line. The 2026 video says fewer than 100 views on 10 videos in a row ([20:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T-F_2Kga3Os&t=1200s)). The 2025 video says "10 videos in a row each video fewer than 50 views" ([13:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I3bOOiJirgk&t=780s)). Both agree that about 200 views means a healthy account.
- "you will be successful guaranteed" ([09:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I3bOOiJirgk&t=571s)). Nobody can promise that.
- "up to 30% of traffic comes from people searching on Tik Tok" ([13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T-F_2Kga3Os&t=810s)). That is her own viral videos only.
- Her fix for one stuck video is to set it private, restart the app, then set it public again ([14:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I3bOOiJirgk&t=841s)). She shows no evidence it works.
- She mentions rage bait and "visually jarring" props to get comments ([18:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T-F_2Kga3Os&t=1082s)). ⛔ That would be wrong for caregivers of people with dementia.
