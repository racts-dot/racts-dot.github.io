## Why this matters to you

Her reason for paying it is sales, stated as a sum. Her app costs about 30 a month, so "if I can get seven customers through 200 Avatar videos then it's worth the investment" ([10:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hqoomzSzsAY&t=600s)).

Run the same sum for your shop. At US$227 a month, how many binder sales pay for it? And an avatar solves being seen. Your 12 Sep reading says people already find you: #2 on Etsy AU for "dementia caregiver binder". What is unproven is what happens after they see the listing. A talking clone does not test that.

# Step 1 — making the clone

Her filming rules, from the April video and repeated in December:

- iPhone front camera, "5 minutes of continuous footage staring directly at the camera" ([3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_jogmHuuKXk&t=181s))
- stay still, few gestures
- exaggerate your intonation a little, because the HeyGen voice flattens it ([3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_jogmHuuKXk&t=181s))
- film outside in shade for natural light ([3:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_jogmHuuKXk&t=211s))
- no cuts: "If you stitch up footage and paste it together, hunen will reject your video." ([3:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_jogmHuuKXk&t=211s))

Then you read a consent statement on camera: "you're going to have to give your consent to hijen" ([4:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_jogmHuuKXk&t=240s)). She sets voice speed to 1.1x ([6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_jogmHuuKXk&t=360s)).

⚠ The title says 1.2M views. The transcript shows single videos of 20,000 to 100,000 views, and she adds "I'm in the AI niche" ([1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_jogmHuuKXk&t=60s)). Views are not sales, and her niche is not yours.

# Step 2 — the automated pipeline

```
Make.com timer, daily
      │
Perplexity: research niche, write 25-second script
      │
OpenAI GPT-4o mini: write caption + hashtags
      │
HeyGen API: make avatar video, 720 x 1280
      │
wait 5 to 8 minutes
      │
fetch video ──► upload to Blotato
      │
router ──► Instagram · Facebook · LinkedIn · Twitter · TikTok · Threads
      └──► Google Sheets row: date, script, caption, video URL
```

Her script prompt is worth keeping: about 25 seconds, sixth-grade reading level, first two sentences say what the video is about, a negative hook, and "never include intermediate thoughts notes or formatting" ([2:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=to1Y42qY6jc&t=150s)).

Two practical notes. On Make's free plan "the execution timeout is five minutes", so the second wait needs a paid plan ([9:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=to1Y42qY6jc&t=572s)). And turn on storing incomplete executions, so a failure can resume ([21:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=to1Y42qY6jc&t=1290s)).

⛔ Perplexity is a paid API. Your rule applies to every run: state the price before the call, and ask at 10c or more. On a daily timer, that is a price per day for ever. None of her videos says what a run costs.

# ⭐ The TikTok "AI generated" label — turn it ON

⛔ The whole video, face and voice, is AI generated. Set it to true. Leaving it off is a platform-terms risk on an account tied to your shop.

# The voice upgrade

HeyGen's own voice is monotone, she says. The free fix is its three API settings: speed 0.5 to 1.5, pitch -50 to 50, and an emotion such as excited ([7:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XMNrTbeMLC0&t=451s)). She found 1.3 speed "way way too fast".

The paid fix is an ElevenLabs clone, linked to HeyGen with an API key. Her professional clone "used four 4 hours of audio" from her YouTube videos ([5:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XMNrTbeMLC0&t=330s)). It needs at least 30 minutes of clean audio and takes "probably 5 six hours" ([15:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XMNrTbeMLC0&t=900s)). The starter plan does not include it ([11:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XMNrTbeMLC0&t=661s)). Her advice is to start with the instant clone, which needs only minutes of audio ([15:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XMNrTbeMLC0&t=930s)).

⛔ A voice clone is the same personal choice as a face clone.

# The background swap

The avatar needs its background removed, which in January needed the Team plan. Behind it plays a video picked at random from a Google Sheet of links. She makes those background videos in Blotato with the voice-over turned off ([5:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hqoomzSzsAY&t=302s)).

Two good testing habits from this video:

- use the cheapest image style, "image with zoom", while testing ([5:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hqoomzSzsAY&t=331s))
- unlink the publishing half until the video half works, so a test never posts ([12:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hqoomzSzsAY&t=721s))

⚠ Her backgrounds are AI images. Fine for a talking-head video. Never let them stand in for pictures of your actual binder pages.

# What to try — one step, tonight

This costs nothing, posts nothing and does not clone you.

```
1. In Claude Code, ask for ONE 25-second script
   for your dementia caregiver binder, using her
   rules: sixth-grade reading level, first two
   sentences say what it is about, a negative
   hook, script only, no notes.
   (Claude on your plan, not Perplexity.)

2. Read it aloud once into Voice Memos on
   your phone. Time it. Face not needed.

3. Write one line under it:
   "Would a caregiver who already found my listing
    buy after hearing this?"
```

That is the smallest free test. It checks whether a spoken pitch says anything your listing page does not. If the answer is no, no avatar will fix it. If yes, the next cheap step is a screen recording of the real binder pages with your voice. No clone, no subscription. ⛔ Posting it anywhere still needs your yes.
