## What to do on it

| Tap this | You get |
|---|---|
| **Items** | all 111 things, newest batch first |
| **All batches** | one row per shopping trip |
| any batch row | a floating box with that batch's items |
| **eBay / Marketplace / Kept** | only those |
| the search box | anything by name, supplier or batch |

Close the floating box by tapping outside it, pressing Escape, or the Close
button.

## Two things worth remembering about this address

**It is on your own website, so anyone who has the link can open it.** It is
hidden from Google, but it is not password protected.

**Buyer names were removed from this copy.** Your costs, your sale prices and
the money tied up in unsold stock are all still on it.
